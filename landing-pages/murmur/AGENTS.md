# Murmur

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional AI phone receptionist. Keep the near-black art direction with the volumetric gradient orb, Instrument Serif headlines with italic gradient words, Figtree body text, native scrolling and the custom shadcn/Base UI controls. Animate numbers with NumberFlow and micro-interactions with Motion; keep them subtle and respect reduced motion. The call console replays scripted, invented calls: never dial, record, play audio or send anything. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus the three demo calls (play, pause, restart, 2× speed, take over), voices, industry tabs, the pricing estimator, FAQ, mobile menu and reduced motion in the browser.
