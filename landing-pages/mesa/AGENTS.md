# Mesa

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional suite of restaurant apps. Keep the warm round-dot halftone art direction: an espresso rounded hero card with a gradient headline, round-dot glows and a live floor plan, thin vertical rails with section dividers, a white floating header pill with an apps menu and a separate black button, a tabbed restaurant site over a full-bleed halftone background that changes colour per app, the hour-by-hour night grid, number panels, the restaurant stories switcher, a dark support band and a giant halftone wordmark in the footer. Local Bricolage Grotesque and DM Mono fonts. Patterns come from `components/halftone.tsx` (canvas, ordered dithering with round dots, paused off screen and for reduced motion). Animate numbers with NumberFlow and micro-interactions with Motion; keep them subtle. Everything runs on invented sample data; never imply a real booking, order or payment. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus the floor plan, apps menu, every tab and module, stories, support desk, FAQ, mobile menu card and reduced motion in the browser.
