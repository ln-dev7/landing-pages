# Tally

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional AI bookkeeper for freelancers. Keep the Swiss style: white paper, a visible 12-column grid, black rules, square corners, no shadows, one signal orange used sparingly, Schibsted Grotesk for type and Martian Mono for labels, flush-left layouts on the grid. Use the custom shadcn/Base UI controls, NumberFlow for numbers and Motion for micro-interactions, and respect reduced motion. The books demo is local: no bank, no upload, nothing stored. Tax figures are estimates, never advice. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus receipt drag-and-drop and tap-to-scan, category changes and ledger totals, the tax pot, the invoice flow, pricing toggle, FAQ, mobile menu and reduced motion in the browser.
