# Hum

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional team chat. Keep the soft grey, ink and magenta art direction shared with Plumb and Redline: dotted hero, white cards with soft shadows, a working product window in the hero with a deep plum sidebar, local Plus Jakarta Sans and Spline Sans Mono fonts, native scrolling and the custom shadcn/Base UI controls. Animate numbers with NumberFlow and micro-interactions with Motion; keep them subtle and respect reduced motion. The workspace runs on invented messages and scripted replies; never imply a real account, and never send what visitors type. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus channels, reactions, threads, sending, huddles, catch-up jumps, the catch-up slider, quiet hours, workflows, active-member pricing, FAQ, mobile menu and reduced motion in the browser.
