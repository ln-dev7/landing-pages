# Murmur

A fictional AI receptionist for small businesses. Murmur picks up every call, books the appointment and texts the owner a summary. Near-black night, a breathing gradient orb and editorial serif type.

## Run

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm dev --port 3194
```

Next.js · React · Tailwind CSS · fully customized shadcn/ui (Base UI). Tabs, FAQ accordion, the pricing slider, voice picker and mobile navigation are keyboard accessible. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences: calls appear complete, with no waveform or orb animation. Fonts are local.

## Interactive demo

The hero holds a live call console. Pick one of three incoming calls (a dental booking, a restaurant table, an emergency plumbing call) and watch it unfold: the orb and waveform follow whoever speaks, the transcript streams in, and every action Murmur takes (checking the calendar, booking, texting, paging the on-call plumber) appears on the right, followed by a summary. Pause, restart, switch to 2× or press Take over. Further down, the voice picker recolours the orb, industry tabs swap the sample conversation and the estimator suggests a plan from your monthly calls.

Businesses, callers, phone numbers (555 range), figures and prices are invented. No call is placed, no audio plays, nothing is recorded or stored.
