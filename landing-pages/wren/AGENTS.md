# Wren

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional AI assistant. Keep the sage grey, ink and teal art direction shared with Plumb and Redline: dotted hero, white cards with soft shadows, a working product window in the hero, Instrument Sans with Source Serif 4 italic accents, local Red Hat Mono, native scrolling and the custom shadcn/Base UI controls. Animate numbers with NumberFlow and micro-interactions with Motion; keep them subtle and respect reduced motion. Answers are pre-written; never imply a real model, account or upload, and never send what visitors type. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus the autoplayed draft, the three prompts, adding risks, versions, the style menu, typed messages, the styles and projects sections, use-case tabs, seat stepper, pricing toggle, FAQ, the closing prompt, mobile menu and reduced motion in the browser.
