# Keel

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional issue tracker. Keep the cool grey, ink and violet art direction shared with Plumb and Redline: dotted hero, white cards with soft shadows, a working product window in the hero, local Onest and IBM Plex Mono fonts, native scrolling and the custom shadcn/Base UI controls. Animate numbers with NumberFlow and micro-interactions with Motion; keep them subtle and respect reduced motion. The workspace runs on invented sample data; never imply a real account, signup or sync. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus every workspace view, status changes, board drag, command menu, Keel Assist, the keyboard section, tabs, member stepper, pricing toggle, FAQ, mobile menu and reduced motion in the browser.
