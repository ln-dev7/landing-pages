# Knot

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional AI automation tool. Keep the neo-brutalist art direction (lavender ground, 2.5px ink borders, hard offset shadows, flat yellow/pink/mint/blue/orange blocks), Bricolage Grotesque and DM Mono, native scrolling and the custom shadcn/Base UI controls. Animate numbers with NumberFlow and micro-interactions with Motion; keep them playful but readable, and respect reduced motion. The canvas is a local demo: nothing connects to real apps and no message is sent. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus the canvas (typing, building, dragging, keyboard moves, inspector switch, test runs on all three recipes), the sentence parser, recipe filters and flips, guardrails, pricing toggle, FAQ, mobile menu and reduced motion in the browser.
