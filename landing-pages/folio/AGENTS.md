# Folio

Standalone Next.js concept for a fictional docs and wiki workspace. Keep the warm paper, ink and emerald art direction shared with Plumb and Redline: dotted hero, white cards with soft shadows, a working product window in the hero, Newsreader serif headings with italic emerald accents, local Hanken Grotesk and Fragment Mono, native scrolling and the custom shadcn/Base UI controls. Animate numbers with NumberFlow and micro-interactions with Motion; keep them subtle and respect reduced motion. The workspace runs on invented pages and pre-written answers built from the page's live state; never imply a real account or sync, and never send what visitors type. Run typecheck and a production build, and verify desktop/mobile layouts plus the page tree, to-dos, block menu, database views, Ask Folio and its sources, block reordering, templates, pricing switches, FAQ, mobile menu and reduced motion in the browser.
